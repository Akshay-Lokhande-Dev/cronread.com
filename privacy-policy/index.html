Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CronRead ("we", "us", "CronRead") handles information when you use cronread.com.

How the tools work

CronRead's cron generator, timestamp converter, JWT decoder, YAML/JSON converter, Base64 encoder, and SQL explainer run entirely in your browser. The cron expressions, timestamps, tokens, or other data you type into these tools are processed locally on your device and are not transmitted to or stored on our servers.

Information we collect

We do not require account creation or login to use CronRead. We may collect limited, non-identifying technical information automatically, such as:

  • Pages visited and general usage patterns (via analytics, if enabled)
  • Browser type, device type, and approximate location (country/region level, via IP address)
  • Referring website, if you arrived from a link

This information is used only to understand how the site is used and to improve it — it is not linked to your identity.

Cookies

CronRead may use cookies or similar local storage technologies for basic site functionality (such as remembering your selected timezone or theme) and, where applicable, for analytics and advertising as described below.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

CronRead may display advertisements served by Google AdSense and other third-party ad networks. These providers may use cookies, web beacons, or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • Google uses advertising cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this site or other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.
  • You can also visit www.aboutads.info to opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising.

For more detail on how Google uses data when you use our site, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Analytics

We may use analytics services (such as Google Analytics) to understand aggregate site traffic and usage. These services may set their own cookies and collect data in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Children's privacy

CronRead is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your choices

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some site functionality but will not prevent you from using the core cron and encoding tools, which run locally in your browser.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
