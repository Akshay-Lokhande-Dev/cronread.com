How AWS EventBridge scheduling works

AWS EventBridge supports two schedule expression types: cron() for calendar-based schedules and rate() for fixed intervals. AWS cron uses six fields: minutes, hours, day-of-month, month, day-of-week, and year.

cron(0 9 ? * MON-FRI *)

# 9:00 AM UTC, Monday through Friday

rate(5 minutes)

# Every 5 minutes

AWS cron uses UTC

EventBridge cron schedules use UTC. If you need a workflow at a local time, convert the target time to UTC before creating the expression. For example, 9:00 AM IST is 03:30 UTC, so a weekday schedule is cron(30 3 ? * MON-FRI *).

For regions affected by daylight saving time, review the UTC conversion when the local offset changes.

AWS cron vs rate expressions

Use rate() when you only care about a repeating interval, such as every 5 minutes or every hour. Use cron() when you need a calendar schedule such as weekdays at 9 AM or the first day of each month.
