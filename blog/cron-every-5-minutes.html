Cron Expression: Every 5 Minutes

The answer, explained, with ready-to-use snippets.

Cron Expression

*/5 * * * *

Runs at minutes 0, 5, 10, 15 … 55 — every hour, every day

What Does */5 * * * * Mean?

Field Value Meaning
*/5MinuteEvery 5 minutes (0, 5, 10 … 55)
*HourEvery hour (0–23)
*Day of monthEvery day
*MonthEvery month
*Day of weekAny day of week

Platform-Specific Snippets

AWS EventBridge

cron(0/5 * * * ? *)

Note: AWS uses 6 fields and requires ? in day-of-week. Standard */5 becomes 0/5.

Kubernetes CronJob

apiVersion: batch/v1
kind: CronJob
metadata:
  name: every-5-minutes
spec:
  schedule: "*/5 * * * *"
  jobTemplate:
    spec:
      template:
        spec:
          restartPolicy: OnFailure
          containers:
          - name: job
            image: your-image:latest

GitHub Actions

on:
  schedule:
    - cron: '*/5 * * * *'

⚠️ GitHub Actions minimum schedule is 5 minutes. Shorter intervals are ignored.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does */5 * * * * run exactly every 5 minutes from when I start it?

No. Cron runs at fixed clock times, not relative to when the job started. */5 * * * * runs at :00, :05, :10, :15 … regardless of when you deploy. If you deploy at 12:03, the first run is at 12:05.

How many times does */5 * * * * run per day?

288 times per day. That's 12 runs per hour × 24 hours = 288 total runs.

What's the difference between */5 and 0/5 in cron?

In standard Unix cron, */5 and 0/5 are identical — both mean "every 5 minutes starting at 0". AWS EventBridge only supports 0/5 syntax (not */5), so use 0/5 in AWS contexts.

Can I run every 5 minutes only during business hours?

Yes: */5 9-17 * * 1-5 runs every 5 minutes from 9am to 5pm, Monday to Friday.
